Rain pattered against the tall library windows, tracing silvery rivers down the fogged glass. The scent of damp earth mingled with the musty perfume of well-loved pages, as golden lamplight softened the heavy shadows that pooled between shelves. Readers nestled in quiet corners, letting the steady drumming from above seep into their solitude, their fingers gliding reverently over spines and stories. Each delicate turn of a page felt like a small rebellion—a gentle defiance of the bleak world beyond, washed in endless grey and alive only in rain-streaked reflection.
