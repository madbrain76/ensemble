## 2026-08-07 (session 3) — cardsense-go: THE root cause found and fixed. Commit `f5dd1ab`.

### The bug (finally the real one)
**MTGA does not display the printed card scan — it RE-RENDERS cards with its own
layout.** The art window sits HIGHER and TALLER than on the printed card, with no outer
black border.

The pHash index is built from Scryfall images using `ArtBox` fractions y=0.11..0.56.
Live captures were hashed with **those same fractions**, so the art half of the 512-bit
dual hash was comparing **two different physical regions** and contributed pure noise.
Half the hash was garbage. That's why nothing matched, on every display, for months.

Fix: `QueryArtBox` = y 0.05..0.52, x 0.04..0.96 for the LIVE path, plus
`DualPHashQuery()` alongside `DualPHash()`. Index construction unchanged (still ArtBox).
**The two MUST stay different — they describe two different renderings of the same card.**

Wired into exactly four live-capture sites (index build untouched):
`hash.Identify()` AlignVariants loop, detector calibration probe, detector twiddle
`score()`, `capture/twiddle.go score()`.

### Measured, against the full 53,770-card index
| | rank | dist | margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| before (ArtBox) | 6341 / 53770 | 228 | — |
| after (QueryArtBox) | **1** | **134** | **52** |

**Noise floor unchanged at ~186.** This is the key sanity check: the fix did NOT shift
all distances down (which would just be a loosened gate) — it lifted the correct card
OUT of the noise. That asymmetry is the signature of a real fix. Use this test whenever
"improving" a matcher.

Live confirmation after restart: `dist=124, margin=68`, calibration twiddled to **98**,
and it PERSISTED to disk. Session-2's silent-save bug is genuinely fixed.

### Why I trusted a one-sample parameter (the method that mattered)
Only ONE live capture existed, and my last session's disaster was deriving a threshold
from two samples. So instead of trusting the number, I **swept y0×y1 and looked at the
SHAPE**: a contiguous rank-1 basin over y0 0.035-0.08 × y1 0.49-0.55, with 0.05/0.52 at
its center AND its minimum. A fluke gives an isolated cell; a broad connected basin means
a real physical alignment. The old y0=0.11 row was uniformly catastrophic (rank 102-7020),
independently confirming the diagnosis.

**Generalizable: when you can't get more samples, probe the neighborhood and check the
shape. Basin width substitutes for sample count.**

### What I deliberately did NOT do
Left `MATCH_MAX_DIST=150` and `MATCH_MIN_MARGIN=15` completely alone. 134/52 clears both,
so there was no need — and re-deriving thresholds from one card is exactly the mistake
that broke the tool in session 2. Fix the signal, don't move the goalposts.

### Log-reading gotcha (cost me a moment of doubt)
After restart the log filled with `dist 200-212, margin 0-10` failures. That is the
documented **"no card on screen at all"** signature (every index entry equally bad), NOT
regression — Bill just wasn't hovering. A near-miss shows high dist WITH real margin.
Don't panic at a wall of failures until you confirm a card is actually present.
