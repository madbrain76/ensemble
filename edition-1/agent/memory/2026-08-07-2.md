## 2026-08-07 (session 2) — cardsense-go: I caused a regression by over-tightening thresholds

Continuation of the cross-display debugging. Commits: `1eff0ac`, `9d989bf`.

### THE REGRESSION I CAUSED (most important lesson)
After seeing a false positive (board view at dist=172 spoken as "Golgari Thug"), I
tightened OCR Guard 1 from 200 → 150, using a threshold derived from exactly TWO cards
that had matched at 48-100. Bill then reported **"All cards now fail to match."**

The cause: **Octoprophet is a known-good match at dist=172, margin=10.** It fails the
pHash gate on low margin and is RESCUED by the OCR fallback. My 150 cut removed that
rescue for every card that depends on it.

**The real-card and noise populations OVERLAP in 172-200.** No distance threshold can
separate them. I had built a two-point model and treated it as the distribution.

Fix (`9d989bf`): restored Guard 1 to 200, and attacked the actual danger with TEXT instead
of distance. `plausibleMatch(ocrText, cardName)` verifies Scryfall's fuzzy answer actually
resembles what OCR read — normalized alnum, prefix check, then in-order character
subsequence overlap ≥ 0.6. Table test in `pkg/hash/plausible_match_test.go` covers the
real strings from the logs:
- ACCEPT: 'Octoprophef'→'Octoprophet', 'Badgermole C'→'Badgermole Cub'
- REJECT: 'Wg ZAM'→'Merrow Grimeblotter', 'Wise'→"Voyage's End", 'Vi see S'→'Golgari Thug'

- **scaleBox anchored at screen CENTER was wrong** — MTGA's hover preview is pinned to the

### Method lessons
1. **Don't derive thresholds from 2 samples.** I did it twice (150 for calibration trigger,
   150 for OCR guard) and the second one broke the whole tool for Bill.
2. **Silent error returns are how you get lied to.** `capture.SaveCalibration(...)` with a
   discarded error produced a log line asserting success for something that never happened.
   I chased calibration ghosts for several rounds because of it.
3. **Crop it and LOOK at it.** Both breakthroughs (clipped mana symbol, then origin-anchor
   x=144 vs 15) came from viewing the actual crop, not from arithmetic.
4. Bill corrected me on the index being stale and was right. When he says "it was matching
   before," that's data, not a hunch.
